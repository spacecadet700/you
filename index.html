The logic

This isn't AI magic. Here's the mechanism.

Every claim on this page reduces to six things OptiOne actually does under the hood.

01 — DETECTION

Statistical anomaly detection

OptiOne doesn't flag every wiggle in a graph. It compares each metric against its own historical variance and only surfaces changes large enough, and sustained long enough, to rule out normal noise.

02 — DIAGNOSIS

Differential diagnosis

When something breaks, OptiOne cross-references the anomaly against every other signal it can see — device, channel, geography, time, segment — ruling out causes one by one until a single explanation survives.

03 — VALIDATION

Controlled experimentation

Before recommending a fix, OptiOne proposes a controlled test — typically the old version against the new one, on a slice of traffic — instead of betting your whole funnel on a guess.

04 — HONESTY

Confidence scoring

Every recommendation ships with a confidence score based on sample size and effect size, plus an expected time to significance — so you can tell a strong signal from an early one.

05 — ACCESS

Native platform access

OptiOne reads, and — with permission — writes, through the same APIs your ad platforms, analytics, and CRM already expose. No new tracking pixels, no rebuilt data layer.

06 — LEARN

A closed feedback loop

Every action is logged against its predicted outcome. Calls that hold up reinforce confidence in similar situations; calls that don't lower it. "Learn" is a scoring loop, not a figure of speech.
